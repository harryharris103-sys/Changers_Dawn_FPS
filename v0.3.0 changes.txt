Changer's Dawn FPS v0.3.0

Graphics presets: the menu button now cycles Low, Balanced and High. Your phone starts on Balanced. If the frame rate drops, the game lowers the setting by itself and tells you.
Lighter drawing:
Walls are drawn with fewer steps.
The floor is drawn at lower detail.
Soldiers are drawn in one piece.
Bots look for targets less often.
Low draws the screen at half the detail.

More 3D

Sky: a dawn sky with sun, clouds, mountains and a city skyline that turns as you look around.
Walls and floor: the walls have shaded edges and fade into the distance, and the floor has a texture.
Soldiers: they now have ground shadows and look darker when far away.
High preset: it adds a dark vignette around the screen edges.

Reload animation

Magazine guns: the gun tilts and lowers, the magazine drops out, your left hand fetches a new one from your belt, it clicks in, and you pull the charging handle before the gun comes back up. The sidearm's slide moves too.
Shotgun and sniper: your hand loads shells one at a time, then works the pump or bolt.
Sounds: each step has its own click or thud, still generated in code.

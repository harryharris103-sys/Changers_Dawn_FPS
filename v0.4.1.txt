v0.4.1. 

What was likely slow: the old version drew the walls one thin slice at a time, about 200 drawing calls every frame. That is fine on a PC but heavy for a Helio A22. It now draws the sky, floor and walls straight into one small picture and puts it on screen once. It looks the same. I also capped how many gun sounds play at once at 8. Phones with a weak processor start on Low, and the game steps the setting down by itself if it still can't keep up.

Things to try on the phone:
Open the file, not the chat preview. Running it inside the Claude app loads a whole extra web page around the game. Download the attached changers-dawn-fps.html and open it in Chrome from your file manager, or open the artifact link in Chrome directly.
Close other apps and turn off battery saver while testing.
Start on the Low graphics setting and raise it only if it runs smoothly.
Use landscape, and don't let the screen record or cast.
Free up some phone storage, because a nearly full phone slows everything down.
